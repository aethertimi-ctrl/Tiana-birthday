# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who contributes to or uses this project.

## Our Standards

- Be kind, respectful, and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on ideas and solutions rather than personal attacks.
- Respect different experiences and perspectives.
- Avoid spam, harassment, discrimination, and abusive language.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments or contributions that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from participation.

## Reporting

Report concerns to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as privately as possible.
